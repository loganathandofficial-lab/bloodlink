❤️ About BloodLink

Technology With
Humanity.

BloodLink is a blood donor management system designed to make donor registration, donor search and blood requests easier to manage.

🩸

Our Mission

BloodLink aims to provide a simple digital platform where registered users can become blood donors, search for donors and submit blood requests.

The system focuses on clear information, straightforward navigation and faster access to donor information.

Our central idea is simple: connecting people when help is needed.

What BloodLink Provides

One Platform. Important Features.

BloodLink brings essential blood management functions together in one website.

🩸

Donor Registration

Registered users can create donor profiles with blood group and location information.

🔎

Donor Search

Users can search registered donors by blood group and city or district.

🚑

Blood Requests

Users can submit blood requests with contact and location details.

Our Values

Built Around People

BloodLink is designed around simplicity, connection and responsible information management.

❤️ Humanity

The purpose of the platform is to support people helping other people through blood donation.

🤝 Connection

BloodLink makes donor and blood-request information easier to connect.

🔒 Responsible Information

The project is designed to keep account and donor information organized within the system.

⚡ Simplicity

The interface is designed so important blood-management actions are easy to understand.
