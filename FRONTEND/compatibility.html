Eight Main Blood Groups

Know the Blood Types

Blood groups are classified according to the ABO and Rh blood group systems.

O+

O Positive

One of the most common blood groups.

O−

O Negative

Commonly known as the universal red-cell donor.

A+

A Positive

A common blood group found in many populations.

A−

A Negative

A less common Rh-negative blood group.

B+

B Positive

A blood group with B antigen and Rh factor.

B−

B Negative

A less common Rh-negative blood group.

AB+

AB Positive

Known as the universal red-cell recipient.

AB−

AB Negative

One of the less common blood groups.

🧬

ABO System

The ABO system classifies blood into A, B, AB and O groups.

➕

Rh Factor

The positive or negative symbol represents the Rh factor of the blood group.

❤️

Why It Matters

Blood compatibility is important when determining suitable blood for transfusion.

⚠️ Important: This compatibility checker is provided for educational and project demonstration purposes. Actual blood transfusion decisions must always be made by qualified healthcare professionals and blood banks using proper testing and medical procedures.
